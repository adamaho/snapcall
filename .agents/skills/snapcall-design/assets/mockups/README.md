# Mockups

## Broadcast direction (October 27, 2026, approved design)

The approved designs live on the [Snapcall art board](https://claude.ai/artifact/Bm6amUamtAUbg4Y7e4T63o), in the "Broadcast" row. They are design references, not native captures; the cards, answers and scores are sample data. The written specs in `references/` win where they differ.

- **Puzzle · Table view**: the hand-strength puzzle in its question state. It is interactive on the art board, so tapping an answer shows the right or wrong state.
- **Puzzle · Wrong answer** and **Puzzle · Right answer**: the same screen after a wrong and a right answer, with the `VerdictPanel` open.
- **Round complete · Stars**: the end-of-round screen at two stars. The board's Tweaks switch it between one, two and three stars.
- **Brand · Broadcast**: the palette, type, parts and principles on one sheet.

An earlier brutalist direction was rejected and removed from the board. Do not revive it. The board's "Type pairings" row shows the fonts that were compared; the final choice (October 27, 2026) is Geist for all interface text, with Instrument Serif only on card ranks.
